# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants must be respectful, technically honest, and welcoming. Do not harass, demean, threaten, expose private information, or misrepresent synthetic results as real evidence. Critique code and claims, not people.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer who shared the project; do not disclose sensitive reports publicly. Once hosted, use the private contact channel identified in repository settings.

Maintainers will review reports promptly, limit disclosure to people needed for resolution, and avoid conflicts of interest. Consequences should be proportionate: correction, warning, temporary restriction, or permanent removal.

## Scope

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere.
